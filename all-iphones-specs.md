# All iPhones — Full Specifications

A GSMArena-style spec sheet for every mainline iPhone released, from the original iPhone (2007) through the iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max (2026). Benchmark/lab-test numbers (AnTuTu, SAR, etc.) are left out since they vary by unit, region and testing house — treat this as a hardware spec reference rather than a review.
